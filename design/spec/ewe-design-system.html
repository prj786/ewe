01 Colour Fluent 2 role names · four states per interactive role

Every interactive role is emitted as a set of four: rest, hover, pressed, selected. A component that needs a hover colour never darkens its own fill — it swaps to the next role in the set, which is why hover feels identical across the bar, the dock, the control centre and the three apps.

Semantic sizes

Labels are governed by three tokens rather than per-component rules: --label-weight: 600, --label-transform: none, --label-tracking: normal. Section headings in the mockup chrome opt out deliberately — 12 px, uppercase, .06em. Anywhere digits line up in a column — meters, load figures, the calendar — set tabular-nums.

03 Icons Lucide, shipped as a subset font

Icons are glyphs in an embedded Lucide TTF subset, not SVG — so they inherit color and font-size and tint with the token layer for free. One rule carries the whole set: .ic { font-family: lucide; font-style: normal; line-height: 1 }.

04 Geometry & space

Radii — four values, by role not by size

The scale is pitched at iOS and then some: a control rounds at 12, a card at 20, a panel at 26, and a button is a true capsule — --radius-pill is 999px, clamped by the browser to half the control's height. Every one of them is a continuous curve: corner-shape: squircle where the engine supports it, a plain radius everywhere else. The curvature is the part that reads as iOS; the numbers alone would just look soft.

Space scale

Fluent's naming, including the two half-steps: snudge at 6 px and mnudge at 10 px, for the gaps where 4 is tight and 8 is loose.

07 Working rules

01Ask for a role, never a value. If a component needs a colour that isn't in the role list, the role list is wrong — adding a literal moves that decision out of the generator, where the next accent change can't reach it.
02The accent is injected at runtime and beats the stylesheet: <html style="--accent: #82aeff">. --accent-default is the fallback for when nothing is injected.
03State comes from the next role in the set, not from opacity or a filter. --bg-1 → --bg-1-hover → --bg-1-pressed → --bg-1-selected.
04Dark only, on purpose. There is no light token set and no prefers-color-scheme branch; the shell paints its own ground at every layer.
05Chrome isn't text. The shell and app frames set user-select: none, with inputs and text areas opted back in — a stray drag shouldn't select a dock label.
06Transitions are 120–170 ms and colour-only. Rail items 120, backgrounds 130–150, chevron rotation 170. Nothing animates layout.
07The apps map roles onto their framework, not the other way round. Tailwind and shadcn aliases are re-pointed at the same tokens — --primary → --brand-bg, --border → --stroke-2, --muted-foreground → --fg-3 — so an app screen and a QML surface resolve to one palette.
08Icons are a font. Sizing and tinting go through font-size and color; there is no per-icon fill to keep in sync.
09Nothing is outlined. Buttons, inputs, selects, cards, tiles, the dock and the window frame all carry zero border width. Every one of them already sits on a different background role than what is behind it, so the layer step separates them; a 1px edge on top of that is a second, redundant signal. Three exceptions earn it: the keyboard focus ring; the 2px accent border on an open control-centre tile; and a 1px inset ring on a window, which steps from --stroke-3 when the window is in the background to --stroke-2 when it has focus — with nothing else distinguishing two overlapping windows, that edge is the only thing carrying which one is active. It is drawn inset rather than as a border so it shares the background's rounding exactly.
10Corners are continuous, not just round. Card and panel surfaces ask for corner-shape: squircle alongside their radius token, so the curve eases in rather than meeting the edge on a tangent. Browsers without it fall back to the plain radius and nothing breaks — never hand-draw a squircle in SVG to fake it. One carve-out: a frame that clips a composited child (a window holding an app) keeps the plain radius, because the squircle clip and the child's own layer disagree at the corner and paint a visible wedge.
